Automatic Blinds

May 06, 2021

A system that automatically opens your blinds in the morning

❮ ❯

During this time, I had wrapped up my first year of undergrad and had already several years of programming experience under my belt. However, it didn't feel like I was a "real" engineer unless I got my hands dirty with some hardware. I purchased my first Arduino kit and after the excitment of getting an LED to blink for the first time had passed, it was time to figure out what my very first project was going to be.

After asking a friend for some inspiration, he shared how his window blinds were opaque since the the street lights were too bright at night. However, this meant that the natural sunlight would not be able to enter when he needed to wake up in the morning either. Interested in the problem, I started to work on creating a system that could automatically open my blinds in the morning.

As a broke univeristy student, I used old RC car and soldered a relay onto the remote which would trigger the motor if enough light hit the photocell. With the use of cardboard, old Bionicle pieces, and hot glue, the system worked! Despite the system being a little janky and impractical due to how loud the motor was, it was a great learning experince.
