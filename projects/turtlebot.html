Turtlebot Mail Delivery

Nov 30, 2022

Deliver mail to the correct rooms

❮ ❯

As part of my introduction to robotics course during my third year of undergrad at the University of Toronto (ROB301), we had the opportunity to work with turtlebots and ROS. Projects included creating a subscriber and publisher node, teleoperating the turtlebot, using a PID controller to follow a line, using a lidar for kalman filtering, and finally, a mail delivery project using bayesian localization.

Each room was represented by an associated colour of paper, connected by hallways represented by black tape. The turtlebot would detect the colour of the room and based on known information about the map, make a prediction using bayesian localization to determine the most likely room it was in. The turtlebot would turn 90 degrees if it reached one of the target rooms to proxy a mail delivery.
