Programmable Table Knocks

May 24, 2021

Detect knocking patterns and program desired actions



As I was thinking about what to do for my next project, I started to reflect on ways in which my day-to-day life could be improved. Since I was working on personal projects constantly, I realized a big part of my workflow was watching tutorials on YouTube while working with my hands. My desk was cluttered with my soldering iron, arduino, wires, and various other tools, meaning that my laptop was often placed on the opposite side of the desk where I was working.

My solution was to implement a system that would allow me to knock my table in a specific pattern to control my laptop. By doing this, I am able to remotely trigger commands (such as pressing the spacebar to pause/play YouTube videos) to my laptop on the other side of the desk.

The system is implemented using an ESP8266 for wireless communication and an accelerometer to detect the knocks. The IMU data is sent to a webserver and is then accessed by a python script running on the laptop which interprets the knocks and triggers a space key press.
