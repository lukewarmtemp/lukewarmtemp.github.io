Deep Learning: anime popularity predictor

Dec 05, 2024

Predict anime rankings based on metadata, synopsis, or thumbnail

Anime-popularity-predictor



As part of my introduction to deep learning course at the University of Toronto, my team and I were tasked with thinking of an project that could use deep learning to solve a problem. After some brainstorming on things that interest us, we decided to train a model that could predict the popularity of new and upcoming anime based on its metadata (airtime, genre, etc.), synopsis, or thumbnail. The idea is that animation companies can use this model to determine whether a new anime is worth producing based on limited information.

The team decided to compare the performance of the model between using metadata, synopsis, and thumbnail as inputs. A standard fully connected neural network was used for metadata, a recurrent neural network for the synopsis, and a convolutional neural network for the thumbnail. The MyAnimeList API was used to gather training, validation, and test data. I personally was responsible for impleneting the recurrent neural network for the synopsis.

Our results indicated that all models were not able to accurately predict the popularity of anime. When plotting the predicted results, we found that the model would always predict a ranking close to the average ranking of all the anime in the dataset. It is possible that there is either no correlation between the inputs and the popularity of the anime, or that the model was not complex enough to capture the relationship. This is because the best way the model could minimize the loss was probably by predicting the average ranking.
