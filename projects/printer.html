Cherry 3D Printer

June 20, 2021

DIY Budget 3D Printer Project

❮ ❯

When attending a youth convension in 2018, I found myself winning a 100 dollar amazon giftcard. From talking with a classmate who sat beside me during highschool computer science class, we started chatting about 3D printers. He mentioned that there was a budget 3D printer that he had built called the Cherry 3D printer, so I decided to build one myself.

Looking back, this project was what first exposed me to arduino and open-source. I got to learn about the mechanical, eletrical, and software components of a 3D printer while getting my hands dirty with cutting wood and metal rods. However, this project took me almost four years to complete. As a highschool student, I think I learned a lot about the frustration of failure through this project. Being hit with lots of roadblocks, I gave up for months at a time and never thought I would ever get it to work. But I did, and I think it taught me a lot about perserverance and problem solving that I still carry with me today as an engineer.
